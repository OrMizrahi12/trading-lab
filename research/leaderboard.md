## How to read this

- A run that passed fewer than 8 rules is not a strategy to trade; the board shows what was scored, not what works.
- Runs from cards were made before the audit log existed and under earlier versions of the judge (for example, the walk-forward rule once tuned a setting that is no longer in the search space). Their rule values are not strictly comparable with audit runs.
- Cards that say the run saw data past the holdout date were judged before the last six months were locked away. Their numbers are not comparable with runs on the current 18-month cache, and they have seen data the final check should be blind to.
- Correlation is measured on the current research cache (18 months), so it says how much two runs overlap, not how they would behave on new data. Card runs are rebuilt on that same cache, so their returns differ from what the card shows.
- Daily returns are rebuilt by one backtest per run at the usual costs. A run whose returns could not be rebuilt (strategy or setting no longer exists) stays on the board unfiltered.
- The research cache only; the holdout is never read.
