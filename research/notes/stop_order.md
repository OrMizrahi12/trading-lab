# Stop order

A stop order is a standing instruction to the broker: "if the price falls to this level, sell at once." It is how a trade caps its loss, for example buying at 100 with a stop at 99 means the plan is to lose about 1 per share at most. Once the price touches the stop, the order becomes a market order (sell at whatever price is available), so the fill can be a little worse than the stop price.
