# Slippage

Slippage is the gap between the price you expected and the price you actually got. If you decide to buy at 100.00 but the order fills at 100.03, the 0.03 is slippage. It happens because prices move while the order travels, and because a big or market order can use up the best offers and have to take worse ones. It is a real cost, so backtests that ignore it look better than live trading would.
